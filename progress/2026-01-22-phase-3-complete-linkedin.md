## Engagement Strategy

**Post timing:**
- Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10am ET (highest B2B engagement)
- Avoid Monday (inbox overload) and Friday (weekend mindset)

**Comment strategy:**
1. **Respond within 1 hour** to every comment
2. **Ask follow-up questions** to keep threads alive
3. **Share technical details** when asked (builds authority)
4. **Link to full blog** for those wanting deeper dive

**Boost engagement:**
- Tag relevant people/companies (but sparingly - 1-2 max)
- Share in relevant LinkedIn groups (AI/ML, SaaS Builders, Indie Hackers)
- Cross-post to personal profile + company page (if applicable)

**Content additions:**
- Add image: Screenshot of Opportunities Dashboard
- Add video: 30-second screen recording of Sanity Check flow
- Add document: Link to full blog post for technical deep dive

LinkedIn allows up to 3 hashtags before looking spammy.

**Alternative sets:**
- `#MachineLearning` + `#Startups` + `#IndieHackers`
