## Posting Tips for LinkedIn:

**Best time to post:**
- Tuesday-Thursday, 8am-10am local time
- Or 12pm-1pm (lunch scroll)

**Engagement strategy:**
- Respond to every comment within first 2 hours
- Ask a question at the end (drives comments)
- Tag relevant connections who might find this useful

**Repost strategy:**
- Share to your feed after 48 hours with new intro
- Cross-post to relevant LinkedIn groups (SaaS founders, indie hackers)

**Hashtag notes:**
- LinkedIn shows first 3 hashtags prominently
- Mix popular (#BuildInPublic) with niche (#TrustInfrastructure)
- Max 5-7 hashtags for professional appearance
