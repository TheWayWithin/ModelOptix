## Brand Personality

### Personality Traits

| Trait | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **Straight-talking** | Direct, no BS, says what others won't | "You're overpaying. Here's proof." |
| **Trustworthy** | Reliable, consistent, keeps promises | "Every recommendation shows its reasoning." |
| **Clever** | Smart but not arrogant, insightful without being condescending | "We monitor 400+ models so you don't have to." |
| **Independent** | Uncompromised, stands apart, answers to no one but users | "No investors. No partners. No agenda." |
| **Helpful** | Genuinely wants users to succeed, not just to sell | "Here's exactly how to switch — and how to roll back if needed." |

### Brand Archetypes

**Primary: The Sage**
The wise advisor who seeks truth and shares knowledge. ModelOptix is the trusted guide helping developers navigate complexity with clarity and insight.

**Secondary: The Outlaw/Rebel**
Challenges the status quo and refuses to play by rigged rules. ModelOptix stands against vendor bias and hidden agendas in the AI industry.

### If Our Brand Were a Person

- **Age:** Mid-30s — experienced enough to know things, young enough to move fast
- **Speaks like:** A senior developer friend who's done the research and is sharing what they found — direct, knowledgeable, zero fluff
- **Dresses like:** Clean and functional — quality hoodie or simple tee, not a suit, not sloppy
- **Values:** Honesty over diplomacy, results over appearances, independence over popularity
- **Would say:** "Look, I tested this myself. Here's what actually works."

## Visual Identity

### Logo

**Logo Concept:** Clean, modern wordmark with a subtle visual element suggesting clarity, optimization, or insight.

**Logo Specifications:**
- Primary Logo: "ModelOptix" wordmark
- Logo Mark: Standalone icon for app icons, favicons
- Clear Space: Minimum padding equal to the height of the "O" in ModelOptix
- Minimum Size: 80px width for digital, 20mm for print

**Logo Usage:**
- Use primary logo on light backgrounds
- Use reversed (white) logo on dark backgrounds
- Never stretch, rotate, or alter proportions
- Never add effects (shadows, gradients, outlines)

### Typography

**Primary Font: Inter**
- Modern, highly legible, free (Google Fonts)
- Optimized for screens — perfect for a developer-focused product
- Wide range of weights for flexibility

| Use | Weight | Size | Example |
|-----|--------|------|---------|
| H1 Headlines | Bold (700) | 32-48px | Page titles |
| H2 Subheadings | Semibold (600) | 24-32px | Section headers |
| H3 Labels | Medium (500) | 18-24px | Card titles |
| Body Text | Regular (400) | 16px | Paragraphs |
| Small/Caption | Regular (400) | 14px | Metadata, hints |

**Monospace Font: JetBrains Mono**
- For code snippets, technical data, API references
- Developer-friendly, highly legible

**Font Links:**
- Inter: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter
- JetBrains Mono: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/JetBrains+Mono

### Imagery Style

**Photography:**
- Style: Minimal, clean, authentic — not stock-y
- Subjects: Real developers, workspaces, screens showing code/data
- Treatment: Natural lighting, muted tones, no heavy filters
- Avoid: Corporate handshakes, staged meetings, generic "AI" imagery (robots, blue circuits)

**Illustrations:**
- Style: Clean line art or simple flat illustrations
- Use: Explain concepts, empty states, onboarding
- Palette: Use brand colors only

**Icons:**
- Style: Outlined (not filled), 1.5-2px stroke weight
- Source: Heroicons (https://heroicons.com) or Lucide (https://lucide.dev)
- Consistency: Use one icon set throughout

**Data Visualization:**
- Use brand colors for charts and graphs
- Savings shown in green, overspend in amber/red
- Clear labels, no chartjunk

## Brand Voice

### Voice Characteristics

| We Are | We Are Not |
|--------|------------|
| **Direct** — Get to the point | Wordy or vague |
| **Honest** — Tell it like it is | Salesy or manipulative |
| **Helpful** — Genuinely useful | Condescending or preachy |
| **Confident** — Know our stuff | Arrogant or dismissive |
| **Clear** — Easy to understand | Jargon-heavy or academic |
| **Human** — Real people talking | Corporate or robotic |

### Tone Variations

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| **Marketing** | Confident, direct, benefit-focused | "Stop overpaying for AI. See what you're missing in 2 minutes." |
| **Product UI** | Clear, helpful, action-oriented | "We found 3 models that could save you money. Review recommendations." |
| **Support** | Warm, patient, solution-focused | "Got it. Let me walk you through how to fix this." |
| **Error Messages** | Calm, clear, helpful | "Something went wrong. Try refreshing, or contact us if it keeps happening." |
| **Social Media** | Casual, engaging, real | "Just found a model that's 40% cheaper with better performance. Love when that happens." |
| **Documentation** | Precise, thorough, scannable | "To add a new model to your stack, click Settings > Models > Add Model." |

### Writing Guidelines

**Do:**
- Lead with the benefit, then explain
- Use "you" and "your" — talk TO the user
- Use specific numbers when possible ("Save 23%", not "Save money")
- Show your reasoning — transparency builds trust
- Admit when you don't know something
- Use contractions (we're, you'll, it's) — sounds more human

**Don't:**
- Use jargon without explanation
- Oversell or hype
- Make promises you can't keep
- Use passive voice when active is clearer
- Be condescending ("Simply click..." implies it should be obvious)
- Use filler words (very, really, actually, just)

### Vocabulary

**Words We Use:**

| Preferred | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| "Recommend" | We advise, we don't demand |
| "Insight" | We provide understanding, not just data |
| "Independent" | Our core differentiator |
| "Your use case" | Personalized, not generic |
| "Save" / "Savings" | Concrete benefit |
| "Optimize" | Active improvement |
| "Transparent" | Our methodology is open |

**Words We Avoid:**

| Avoid | Why | Use Instead |
|-------|-----|-------------|
| "Cheap" | Sounds low-quality | "Cost-effective" or "saves money" |
| "Best" | Subjective and generic | "Best for your use case" |
| "Revolutionary" | Overused tech hype | "Different" or specific benefit |
| "Synergy" | Corporate jargon | Just say what you mean |
| "Leverage" | Jargon | "Use" |
| "AI-powered" | Meaningless in 2026 | Describe what it does |

## Application Examples

### Do's

- Use the full logo with adequate clear space
- Maintain color contrast ratios (4.5:1 minimum for text)
- Keep messaging direct and benefit-focused
- Show your work — include data sources and reasoning
- Use the primary CTA color (Deep Navy) consistently

### Don'ts

- Don't place logo on busy backgrounds
- Don't use colors outside the palette
- Don't write in corporate-speak or use buzzwords
- Don't make claims without backing them up
- Don't use stock photos of robots or circuit boards
