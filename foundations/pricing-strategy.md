## Pricing Principles

**1. The 10x/10x Gate**
I only build tools that are 10x better AND 10x cheaper than alternatives. If I can't hit both, I don't build it.

**2. Week One Payback**
Annual subscription cost recovered in your first week. After that, it's pure profit.

**3. Exponential Returns**
Savings compound—not linear. More products, more optimizations, growing margins. Many users see 1000x return in year one.

**4. Lean = Your Gain**
No office, no staff, no investors. AI-first automation. My low overhead becomes your 10x value.

**5. Independence, No Hidden Fees**
Subscriptions only. No volume incentives, no referral fees, no provider kickbacks.

**6. Prove Value Before Trust**
7-day trial available as fallback. Money-back guarantee on all plans. Experience it first, decide second.

For users who choose the 7-day trial:
- Full Solo features during trial
- Credit card upfront

## Objection Handling

### "Too expensive"

**For Solo users:**
> "At $75/hour, 2 hours of research costs more than a year of Solo."

**For Growth/Pro users:**
> "One delayed model switch costs $1000s/year in overspend. I catch that in week one, not month six."

**For competitive builders:**
> "Your competitors are optimizing. If you're running 6-month-old model choices while they're on this week's best option—that's not a cost problem, it's a survival problem."

### "I can do this myself"

> "You can—but should you? Every hour researching models is an hour not building your product. I do the grunt work so you decide faster."

### "Not sure I need ongoing monitoring"

> "New models launch monthly, pricing changes weekly. What was best 6 months ago often isn't now. This is protection against the landscape changing under you."

### "What if recommendations are wrong?"

> "Every recommendation shows its reasoning. Plus sanity-check testing lets you validate with your actual prompts before switching. You're never flying blind."

### "I'll wait until I need it"

> "Developers usually wait until mid-project, under deadline, then realize they picked wrong. The trial is free—have answers ready before you need them urgently."

### "My current setup works fine"

> "Working fine today isn't the same as winning tomorrow. In AI, standing still means falling behind. The question isn't whether your stack works—it's whether it's the best it could be while your competitors are optimizing theirs."
